Satellite and airborne remote sensing &
Optical and thermal imagery, aerosol products and plume products &
Broad-area spatial coverage; timing and usable vertical information depend on the product and overpass &
Cloud and smoke obscuration, revisit delay, retrieval assumptions, and limited near-surface or vertical information &

Crewed and community mobile sensing &
Instrumented service and mapping vehicles, bicycles, and citizen low-cost sensors~\citep{degroot2025snuffelfiets} &

Route bias, calibration, maintenance, operator dependence, and uneven geographic coverage &
Primarily adjacent-domain transfer evidence \\

UAV particle and gas sampling &
Airborne particle sampling and gas measurements; representative systems reviewed by \citet{jonca2022drone} &
Adaptive local and vertical sampling close to a plume; measurement rate depends on payload and sensor &
Endurance, payload, weather, positioning, calibration, communication, and incident airspace &
Mixed fire-specific sampling and adjacent-domain transfer evidence \\

Structured industrial-plume UAV sensing &
High-frequency airborne gas measurement and localisation in evaluated industrial and controlled plumes~\citep{chen2023using,soskind2023stationary} &
Examples include 1~Hz gas measurements and configuration-specific localisation performance &
Transferability to wildfire chemistry, turbulence, safety, and incident operations is unvalidated &
Transferred methodological evidence \\

Reconfigurable UAV platform &
LiDAR and 640$\times$512 thermal imaging in the evaluated PULSAR configuration~\citep{perikleous2024novel} &

Payload-endurance trade-off, weather, BVLOS and airspace, and task-specific sensor suitability &
Platform-capability evidence; not end-to-end fire-response validation \\
